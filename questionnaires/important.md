# Important Vendor Questionnaire

> [!NOTE]
> Draft. Not yet reviewed.

**Purpose.** About 15 questions. The critical questionnaire's essentials, minus the depth that only matters when the blast radius is total. If a vendor's answers here raise eyebrows, promote them to the [critical questionnaire](critical.md) rather than arguing with the tier.

## Governance

1. Who owns security at your company, and what attestation or certification do you hold (current copy requested below)?
2. Have you had a security incident affecting customer data in the last three years? What happened, and what changed?
3. When did your last penetration test or independent security assessment happen?

## Data Handling

4. What data of ours do you hold, where is it stored, and is it encrypted in transit and at rest?
5. Is MFA enforced for all staff access to systems holding our data?
6. How is staff access granted and removed, and how fast on departure?
7. Do you use our data for anything beyond delivering the service, including AI training?
8. Which subprocessors touch our data?
9. When we leave, how does our data come back and get deleted?

## Incident Response

10. Do you have a documented incident response plan?
11. How fast will you notify us of an incident affecting our data, and will you put that number in the contract?
12. Who do we call, name and channel, when something looks wrong on your side?

## Evidence Requested

13. Current SOC 2 or equivalent report, read for [scope and exceptions](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/Security-Program-Starter/blob/main/mappings/soc2.md), not just the logo.
14. Proof of cyber insurance.
15. The breach notification commitment, in writing, once, so the contract conversation starts from paper instead of memory.

Score it with [scoring.md](../scoring.md). One follow-up call at most; if it needs more than one, that is tier information, not diligence.
